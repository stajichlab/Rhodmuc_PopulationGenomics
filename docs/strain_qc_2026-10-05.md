## Points for review

1. **The `low_coverage` rule drops 6 good *R. mucilaginosa* strains.**

   | Strain | DP<5 fraction | mosdepth mean | Genome ≥ 5× | Reads mapped | Mismatch |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | TFCN_137D-4 | 0.862 | 18.5× | 99% | 95.5% | 0.29% |
   | TFCN_17-333D-2 | 0.529 | 19.5× | 99% | 97.5% | 0.21% |
   | TFCN_25-0-2E332-1 | 0.563 | 27.7× | 99% | 98.2% | 0.19% |
   | TFCN_25-337M-3 | 0.830 | 16.5× | 99% | 98.7% | 0.20% |
   | TFCN_363-1-2 | 0.557 | 18.5× | 98% | 96.4% | 0.56% |
   | TFCN_89D-3 | 0.545 | 17.1× | 98% | 96.6% | 0.52% |

   The cause is the DP that GATK reports for a hom-ref genotype. It comes from the gVCF reference block, which records the block's minimum depth, not the depth at the site. On CM179498.1:
   - TFCN_363-1-2 has a median genotype DP of 4. mosdepth gives 18.5×.
   - TFCN_209-6-1 has a median of 10. mosdepth gives 34×.
   - 99.9% of TFCN_363-1-2's DP < 5 genotypes are hom-ref.

   So the rule measures the gVCF band structure more than the read depth. Options:
   - (a) Keep the rule as it is. The 6 strains stay out.
   - (b) Compute `frac_lowq` on non-ref calls only, or replace it with the mosdepth fraction of the genome at ≥ 5×.
   - (c) Keep the rule, but add these 6 as reviewed exceptions.

   Any change also affects which hom-ref genotypes count in downstream filters. VARIANT_QC_FILTER uses `FMT/DP<5`, so these strains would still lose many hom-ref calls there.
2. **No-metadata strains that genotype as *R. mucilaginosa*.** TFCN_1A-1-5, TFCN_2M-1-3, TFCN_86C-3 and TFCN_25-332M-2 are clean haploids: sourmash 1.000, het 0. They are dropped only because `metadata.txt` has no row for them. NRRL_Y-2510 has the same status but is included through `INCLUDE_NO_METADATA`. Add metadata rows for them, or add them to `INCLUDE_NO_METADATA`?
3. **The *R. aff. mucilaginosa* × *R. mucilaginosa* hybrids TFCN_17-333M-1 and TFCN_186CL-2** are diploid with het 0.25, but they have no metadata, so they are dropped. Should they go into `hybrid_diploids`?
4. **DBVPG_4952** is a kept hybrid diploid with a *frigidialcoholis*-leaning partner. The sourmash call for its 9003 library (excluded) pointed the same way.
